Business rules

  • Every account belongs to exactly one subclass (and through it one class) and exactly one institution. Accounts with no outside party use the Internal institution (00).
  • An account's code is class initial + subclass number - institution number - account suffix, for example L01-42-2946. The suffix is the last 4 digits, or XXXX/YYYY when there's no number.
  • Assets and Expenses have a normal debit balance; Liabilities, Capital and Revenue have a normal credit balance.
  • A subclass on the balance sheet is current or long-term; income statement subclasses have no term.
  • Each journal entry line is either a debit or a credit, never both. Amounts are whole cents.
  • A journal entry can be posted only when it has at least 2 lines and total debits equal total credits. Once posted it's locked; mistakes are fixed with a correcting entry.
  • Each journal entry is linked to its source documents. The document it was recorded from, and any document used to check it. The same statement line can be recorded only once.
