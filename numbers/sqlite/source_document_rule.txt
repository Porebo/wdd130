SOURCE DOCUMENT RULE
Lester Carcamo household books
Adopted 2026-09-24

============================================================
PURPOSE
============================================================
The same transaction often appears on two statements. For example,
a credit card payment shows on the card statement AND on the bank
statement. It is ONE transaction and must be recorded ONCE.

This rule says which statement each kind of transaction is recorded
from. The other statement is used only to check it.


============================================================
THE RULE
============================================================

1. CREDIT CARD STATEMENTS
   Record:  purchases, fees, interest, and refunds or returns
            (everything except payments)
   Check:   payments (they are recorded from the bank statement)

2. BANK STATEMENTS
   Record:  every line - payments to cards and loans, deposits
            (paychecks, rideshare), withdrawals, and transfers
   Check:   nothing is skipped; every line is recorded or matched

3. LOAN AND MORTGAGE STATEMENTS
   Record:  nothing on their own
   Use for: the split of each payment into principal, interest
            and escrow. The payment itself comes from the bank
            statement (date and total); the lender statement
            supplies the split lines of that same entry.


============================================================
SPECIAL CASES
============================================================

Transfers between your own accounts (for example checking to savings)
   Record once, from the statement of the account the money LEFT.
   Check it on the statement of the account it arrived in.

Refunds and returns on a card
   Record from the card statement. They lower the card but no money
   moves through the bank.

Split deposits (one paycheck into several accounts)
   Record once, as one entry with one line per account.

Dates that differ between statements
   A payment can show on the bank one or two days apart from the card.
   Match on amount and payee, allowing a few days' difference. Use
   the bank date as the entry date.


============================================================
EXAMPLE - Target Circle Card statement closing 2026-08-10
============================================================

From the card statement, record:
   2026-08-09  purchase   2.79    debit expense, credit L01-24-6343
   2026-08-09  purchase 118.69    debit expense, credit L01-24-6343

From the card statement, only check:
   2026-08-06  autopay   30.00    (recorded from the bank statement)

From the Bank of America statement, record:
   2026-08-06  autopay   30.00    debit L01-24-6343, credit A01-35-7632

The one autopay entry lowers the card AND lowers checking.


============================================================
OPENING BALANCES (CUT-OFF)
============================================================

The books start on 2026-07-30.
   * Each account opens at its balance at the end of 2026-07-30,
     recorded against C01-00-0001 Lester Carcamo, Capital.
       Asset opening:     debit the asset,       credit Capital
       Liability opening: debit Capital,         credit the liability
   * Only transactions AFTER 2026-07-30 are recorded.
   * When a statement straddles the cut-off, work out the balance on
     2026-07-30 from the statement's transaction list.
     Example: Target card 177.11 previous balance + 60.03 purchase
     - 177.11 payment (all before 7/30) = 60.03 opening balance.


============================================================
RECONCILIATION CONTROL
============================================================

After both sides of a statement period are entered, the books must
equal the statement:

   Each bank account   books balance = bank statement ending balance
   Each credit card    books balance = card statement ending balance
                       (only after the bank statement is also entered)

If a card does not match, look first for a payment still missing from
the bank side. If a bank account does not match, look for a line not
yet recorded or recorded twice.

Example: Target card on 2026-08-10
   60.03 opening - 30.00 autopay + 2.79 + 118.69 = 151.51
   151.51 = statement ending balance  -> reconciled
