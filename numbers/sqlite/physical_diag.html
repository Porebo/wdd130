Views and their joins

ViewBaseJoinsOutput
accounts_chart_view account a INNER JOIN account_subclass s ON s.subclass_id = a.subclass_id
INNER JOIN account_class c ON c.class_id = s.class_id
INNER JOIN institution i ON i.institution_id = a.institution_id
LEFT JOIN account_term t ON t.term_id = s.term_id 		Builds account_code = class_initial + subclass_number - institution_number - account_suffix (for example L01-42-2946), plus names, normal balance, term and sort orders
journal_view journal_entry_input e INNER JOIN journal_entry_type t ON t.journal_entry_type_id = e.journal_entry_type_id
INNER JOIN journal_entry_line l ON l.journal_entry_input_id = e.journal_entry_input_id
INNER JOIN accounts_chart_view v ON v.account_id = l.account_id 		One row per line, with entry_number JE-000001, date, type, account code and name, and amounts in dollars
